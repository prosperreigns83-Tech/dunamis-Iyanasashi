About Dunamis Church

Dunamis Church is a Christ-centered, Bible-believing church located in Iyanosashi. We are passionate about raising men and women who live by faith, walk in holiness, and operate in the power of the Holy Spirit.

Our Vision

To raise a people of spiritual authority, godly character, and kingdom influence who will impact their generation for Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

• Teaching the undiluted Word of God
• Building a strong prayer culture
• Discipling believers for spiritual maturity
• Winning souls and expanding God’s kingdom
• Raising godly families and leaders
